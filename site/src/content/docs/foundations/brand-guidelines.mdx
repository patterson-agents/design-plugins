---
title: Brand guidelines
description: Eighteen browser-openable specimen cards covering color, type, logo, voice, and shape — the Patterson brand, rendered.
---

Each card below is a real, self-contained HTML file from the `patterson-brand`
plugin's `ds/guidelines/` folder. Open one and you're looking at the same
markup a plugin ships — no build step, no screenshot standing in for the real
thing.

## Color

| Card | What it shows |
|---|---|
| [Brand palette](/plugins/patterson-brand/ds/guidelines/brand-palette.card.html) | The primary palette — navy, sky, gray, white — with hex, PMS, and role. |
| [Primary colors](/plugins/patterson-brand/ds/guidelines/colors-primary.card.html) | Navy `#003767` and sky `#00A8E1` in their headline and CTA roles. |
| [Secondary colors](/plugins/patterson-brand/ds/guidelines/colors-secondary.card.html) | Blue, light blue, and light gray — for depth, never dominant. |
| [Tertiary colors](/plugins/patterson-brand/ds/guidelines/colors-tertiary.card.html) | Green, teal, and purple — reserved for infographics and decks. |
| [Neutral ramp](/plugins/patterson-brand/ds/guidelines/colors-neutral.card.html) | The published gray steps used for body copy, borders, and surfaces. |
| [Semantic colors](/plugins/patterson-brand/ds/guidelines/colors-semantic.card.html) | Success, info, warning, and danger — the shipped Digital Pattern Library states. |
| [Tints](/plugins/patterson-brand/ds/guidelines/colors-tints.card.html) | The 75/50/25 tint ramp over white for every palette color. |

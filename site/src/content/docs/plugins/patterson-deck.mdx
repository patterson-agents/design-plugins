**Tag:** Template

Patterson deck scaffolds a full 16:9 company deck: cover, section divider, stats, comparison, quote, capabilities table, full-bleed photo band, and closing slides, all carrying the wave watermark and official brand imagery. It's static HTML — no build step, no framework — and it prints straight to PDF, one page per slide.

Reach for it any time a request calls for a deck, a business review, or a company overview in front of an audience. The template is nine archetypes deep, so most decks are an exercise in duplicating a `<section>` and swapping copy, not designing from scratch.

The rules stay visible in the markup itself: keep `data-label` on every section, white logo on navy slides and navy logo on light ones, big sky numbers for stats, a 24px text minimum, and sentence-case headings throughout. The deck-builder agent picks the right archetype for the content and keeps the copy on-voice as it writes.
